The quick answer

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference that organizes product links and comparison notes into a scannable list. Useful entries include a category, measurements, material details, a source link and the date the information was checked.

This page is an independent category guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Kakobuy service. Use its CAFANS links to browse, then verify each listing and any shopping-agent requirements separately.

03 / A practical browsing method

Turn finds into a useful shortlist.

A Kakobuy shopping guide starts with consistent comparisons, not a long list of unchecked links.

01.

Choose the item type.

Start with one category and a clear use case. For Kakobuy shoe finds, note the intended fit and style. For Kakobuy clothing finds, decide which measurements and fabric characteristics you need before comparing listings.

02.

Record the evidence.

Save the source link, listed specifications, size chart and date checked. Keep seller claims separate from details you can verify. Photos can help compare construction, but they do not establish authenticity on their own.

03.

Check the complete cost.

Compare item price alongside delivery, service fees and applicable taxes. For Kakobuy accessories finds, record dimensions and materials too. Confirm current ordering and return conditions with the relevant provider.

04 / Keep the useful details

What belongs in your product comparison?

Use the same fields for every candidate so that differences are easier to spot.

Suggested fields for a personal shopping shortlist
Category Record these details Before you decide
Shoes Size system, listed foot-length guidance, upper material and outsole details. Use the specific listing's size chart; do not rely on a size conversion alone.
Tops & jackets Chest, shoulder, sleeve and garment length; composition and lining. Compare with a garment you own and confirm whether measurements are flat or circumferential.
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening and stated stretch. Check the measurement method and whether the waistband is adjustable.
Headwear Circumference range, crown depth, brim dimensions and fastening. Confirm the adjustment range instead of assuming that one size will fit.
Jewelry & accessories Dimensions, stated metal or fabric, closure type and care instructions. Verify material claims, especially if you have a known sensitivity.
Every item Source URL, date checked, price, currency, delivery estimate and return terms. Refresh the information before paying; listings and service conditions can change.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference that organizes product links and comparison notes into a scannable list. Useful entries include a category, measurements, material details, a source link and the date the information was checked. This page is an independent category guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Kakobuy service.

Where do the category links on this page go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing links open the CAFANS homepage. They do not open a Kakobuy checkout.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item that fits you. Check the measurement method and units. Chest width, shoulder width, garment length and sleeve length are more useful than a size letter alone.

How do I estimate the total purchase cost?

Consider the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Obtain current quotes from the relevant seller or service before paying.

Do the brand illustrations verify authenticity or stock?

No. The Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès visuals are stylized editorial illustrations, not product photographs or verified listings. They do not establish authenticity, availability, brand affiliation or authorization.

What should I check before using a shopping agent?

Confirm the service's current ordering process, supported products, fees, inspection options, return conditions and shipping destinations directly with the provider. Do not assume that a product link is supported or that a return will be available.
